# PillPlan

Build your daily medication schedule from your wake time: evenly spaced dose times, food and water reminders, and a printable day plan. Free tool.

## Features
- Doses-per-day converted to evenly spaced times from your wake hour
- Per-dose food and water reminders attached to the schedule
- Printable one-page day plan for the fridge or a carer
